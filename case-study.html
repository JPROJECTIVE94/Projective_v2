econergy.net

Chicago based Econergy has helped more than 2,500 schools and businesses cut their energy bills by pooling them into buying coalitions, and since 2018 it has run the Future Green Energy Consortium for Illinois school administrators, school boards and business officials. Its model asks nothing of a district up front: Econergy carries the cost, the school keeps the savings. When it set out to put solar on those same schools, it brought projects as strong as any in the country: [25 year] PPAs, Illinois Shines REC revenue, and offtakers rated A to AA by Moody’s with the State of Illinois behind them. Cheaper power for public schools, backed by public credit.

The projects were shovel ready. The capital was not. Each sat in the $2 to 5M range, below the radar of project finance desks and beyond the reach of community banks. A bank construction loan takes four to six months. The ITC deadline left weeks. Good American infrastructure was about to stall for lack of a lender who could keep up.

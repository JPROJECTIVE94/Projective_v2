What we did

One upload, one review, one facility.

Econergy uploaded its project documents to Projective. We reviewed the credit, EPC terms, module sourcing and takeout at the commercial operating date (COD), then structured a construction facility funded from Projective’s lending pool. Capital was committed in four weeks. The builders never stopped building.

01 · Intake
Upload the deal

Model, PPAs, EPC and supply contracts, interconnection, tax structure. One upload, one data room, every deal in your pipeline in the same place.

02 · Review
15 to 20 days

Projective’s agents read every document against our underwriting standards: offtaker credit, EPC price certainty, module sourcing and FEOC exposure, takeout path at the commercial operating date (COD). Our credit team confirms the output, completes KYB, and issues terms on deals that clear.

03 · Structure
One vehicle

Twelve-month senior construction facility, first lien, drawn against independent engineer certification. Multiple sites from the same builder close as a single vehicle on a single ledger: one set of documents, one set of fees.

04 · Fund
Milestone draws

Capital comes from Projective’s lending pool. Each draw is recorded as it happens, so lenders see every dollar and every milestone in real time, not in a quarterly report.

05 · Takeout
Commercial operating date (COD)

At commercial operation the facility is repaid through tax credit transfer and term debt. The builder moves to the next project; the pool recycles into the next site.
